Welcome

Grange Prestonfield Community Council (GPCC) comprises elected local residents and appointed representatives of constituted local interest groups. Together, they work to represent your local community and its neighbourhoods, putting forward your views on your 'place', 'space', amenity and wellbeing to the City Council and to other public agencies.

Roles & Responsibilities of GPCC

1

Statutory consultation

GPCC has the statutory right to be consulted by the City Council on local matters, including certain planning applications and licensing decisions.

2

Representing residents

Elected councillors and appointed local group representatives put forward residents' views on neighbourhood and city issues that affect the area.

3

Open, monthly meetings

GPCC meets monthly and every meeting is open to all local residents, with details of forthcoming meetings posted on this site.

Our Community Councillors

GPCC presently has 14 members, appointed by public election in March 2025. Anyone 16 years or over who is a resident in the GPCC area can stand for election, or be co-opted if a vacancy occurs during the four-year term.
